FAQ

ANSWERS, HONESTLY.

How do I reserve? ＋

Request your dates on the Reserve page or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, then secure your reservation with a deposit.

What payment methods are accepted? ＋

Deposits and balances are processed through the configured payment provider at launch (no fake checkout). Details are confirmed with every reservation.

What is the cancellation policy? ＋

Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. The deposit policy inside 7 days is confirmed at booking.

Are children welcome? ＋

Yes — families are at the heart of the ranch. Children policies for day passes and stays are confirmed before your visit.

How far is the ranch? ＋

Near Etzatlán, between Guadalajara and Tequila. Exact times depend on route — ask us and we will send the current one.

Can I visit for the day? ＋

Yes, with a day pass when available. Check hours and pricing on this page before you come.

Only confirmed answers are published. Unverified details are omitted, not guessed.
