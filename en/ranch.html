A PLACE WITH MEMORY.

A historic property with roots in the early 1800s, cared for by the Bañuelos family across generations.

The story

THE LAND. THE FAMILY. THE GENERATIONS.

Rancho El Aguacero stands on land worked and loved for over two centuries. The aqueduct, the patios, the trees — each carries the memory of the people who built and kept this place.

Today the Bañuelos family opens the ranch to guests: to rest, to gather, to celebrate. Nothing here is staged. The history is in the walls, the shade, the water.

Historical details are published only as verified by the family. Unverified details are intentionally omitted.
